# Sacred script opcodes -- behavioural profile

Generated by `analysis/tools/binary/opsem.py`. Companion to `script-opcodes.md`, which carries the static side (handler address, strings, RTTI classes).

`tag sig` is the most common operand tag sequence and the share of records that use it -- 1.0 means the opcode has exactly one shape. `res text` categorises the longest string its `res:N` operands resolve to in
`global.res`, with its length. The text itself is the game's own copyrighted
writing and is deliberately not reproduced here -- the category and length are
what identify the operand as text rather than a resource id.

NINE OPCODES ARE ABSENT HERE ON PURPOSE: 10, 11, 12, 13, 19, 47, 61, 77 and 110 have live cases in the dispatcher but ZERO records in the shipped scripts, so the corpus says nothing about them. Opcode 42 is the mirror image -- 4,950 records, no case.

CAVEAT ON `res:`: the same prefix addresses BOTH global.res text slots and object/creature resources, so a short or nonsensical resolution on a spawn opcode (1, 8) means the operand was never text. Median length separates the two.

TAGS 0x48/0x49/0x4a/0x5d/0x5e/0x6d/0x6e/0x7a WERE FIXED: their handlers (0x0826cb0e, 0x0826de28) store a u32 in the numeric slots and THEN strcpy a string, so they carry BOTH. They had been typed STR, which ate the u32 as the string and parsed the rest of the record from the wrong offset. Records consuming their body exactly went from 95.6% to 99.774% (opcode 40 excluded -- its 80-byte struct is not a tag stream).
